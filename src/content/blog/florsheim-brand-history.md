# Florsheim Brand History

At Florsheim, they take immense pride in their rich brand history that spans over a century. Since their inception in 1892, they have been at the forefront of the footwear industry, blending timeless craftsmanship with contemporary designs. Their commitment to quality and style has made them a trusted name in the world of men's shoes.

## Origins and Founding

The Florsheim story began in **1892 when Milton Florsheim,** a young entrepreneur with a passion for quality footwear, opened his first factory in **Chicago, Illinois**. His vision was clear – to craft shoes that embodied the perfect blend of style, durability, and comfort. With an unwavering dedication to excellence, Florsheim quickly gained recognition for its superior craftsmanship and attention to detail.
