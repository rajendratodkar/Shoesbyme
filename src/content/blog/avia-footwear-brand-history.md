Avia was founded by Jerry Stubblefield and Carl McCowan in 1979 with a focus on providing high-quality running shoes. Originally focused on the athletic market, Avia has evolved into a lifestyle brand that offers a wide range of footwear for men, women, and children. The brand is known for its commitment to performance, comfort, and style in every pair of shoes it creates.

Over the years, the brand has expanded into the casual footwear market and has become known for its innovative design, technology, and style. Today, Avia is a leading footwear brand that offers a wide range of products for all ages and lifestyles.

## The Early Years: Focused on Fitness

> Jerry Stubblefield and Carl McCowan established the company in 1979 in Prague, Czech Republic.

### Avia’s Origin as a Running Shoe Brand

Avia was originally founded as a running shoe brand, with a focus on providing high-quality shoes for athletes. The brand’s founders, Jerry Stubblefield and Carl McCowan, recognized the need for shoes that offered both performance and comfort, and set out to create a brand that would meet those needs.

### The Importance of Performance and Comfort in Avia’s Footwear

From the beginning, performance and comfort were at the forefront of Avia’s footwear design. The brand’s shoes were developed with the latest technology and materials to ensure that they met the demands of athletes. Avia’s shoes were also designed with comfort in mind, using cushioning and support to keep feet comfortable during intense workouts.

### The Development of Avia’s Footwear Technology

Over the years, Avia has continued to innovate and develop its footwear technology. The brand has introduced new materials, cushioning systems, and support features to enhance the performance and comfort of its shoes. Avia’s footwear technology has made it a leader in the athletic footwear market and has helped to establish the brand as a trusted name in performance footwear.

## The Shift towards Fashion and Lifestyle

### Avia’s Expansion into the Casual Footwear Market

As Avia’s reputation for performance and comfort grew, the brand began to expand into the casual footwear market. Avia started offering more versatile and stylish options that could be worn both in and out of the gym. This expansion allowed the brand to reach a wider audience and appeal to those who wanted high-quality footwear for everyday wear.

### The Evolution of Avia’s Design Aesthetic

As Avia moved into the casual footwear market, the brand also began to develop a more fashion-forward design aesthetic. Avia’s footwear started to feature modern, sleek, and stylish designs that not only offered comfort but also made a fashion statement. This shift towards fashion allowed Avia to compete with other lifestyle brands and appeal to a wider range of customers.

### The Introduction of Avia as a Lifestyle Brand

With its expansion into the casual footwear market and its focus on fashion, Avia has become a well-known lifestyle brand. Today, Avia offers a wide range of products for all ages and lifestyles, from performance athletic footwear to stylish casual shoes. The brand has become synonymous with quality, comfort, and style, and continues to innovate and evolve with the changing fashion trends.
