Here are some tips to help you find the perfect pair of hiking shoes. First and foremost, comfort is key. You're going to be doing a lot of walking (or even running) in these shoes, so make sure they're comfortable from day one.

## What are hiking shoes?

Hiking shoes are a type of footwear designed to provide comfort and traction on rough terrain. Hikers need specialized footwear to protect their feet from the elements and reduce the risk of injury.

Most hiking shoes have a waterproof outer layer to keep your feet dry in wet conditions, and a breathable inner layer to allow your feet to stay cool in warm weather.

## Type of Hiking Footwear

### Hiking Shoes
When selecting a pair of shoes, the terrain should take priority.

### Light Hiking Shoes
A pair of hiking shoes with a snug fit, are light, and have flexible soles should work if the trail is short and in a forested area without significant climbs.

### Backpacking Boots
If you will be trekking over difficult terrain for a considerable amount of time while carrying a light backpack, consider investing in somewhat hefty hiking boots with strong, flexible soles.

### Mountaineering Boots
These are for long hikes when you're hauling a big backpack. They are thicker than hiking boots and extend well above the ankle. Wear them consistently for at least a fortnight prior to the hike.

## Tips to help you choose right hiking shoes

### Select right Size
Make sure they're the right size both length and width. Your shoes should be snug but not too tight, with enough room to wiggle your toes.

### Check the Materials
Look for shoes made of breathable materials like leather or canvas. Avoid synthetic materials that are more likely to cause blisters.

### Look for comfortable linings
Choose shoes with soft, padded linings to help prevent blisters and ensure a more comfortable fit.

### Pay attention to the heel
The heel should be snug but not too tight, and cushioned to protect your feet from impact.

### Make sure soles are grippy
The soles should have good traction to prevent slipping and falling.

### Consider the climate and terrain
You'll want different shoes for different conditions. Consider waterproof options for wet weather and insulated options for colder temperatures.

## Care Tips for Hiking Shoes

1. Clean all dirt and debris off after each use
2. Use mild soap and warm water to wash nylon and mesh uppers
3. Let wet boots dry in a warm area with newspaper inside
4. Clean leather boots with gentle leather cleaner and condition them

## Conclusion

A half size to a size larger is adequate room for foot swelling during long-distance hiking. Buy boots that are at least one to two sizes bigger to support the swelling in your feet.
