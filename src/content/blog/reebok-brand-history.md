Reebok is a global athletic footwear and apparel company that was founded in 1895 in the United Kingdom. The brand has a rich heritage and has been a major player in the athletic shoe market for decades.

## Early Years

Reebok began as a small company focused on creating running shoes. They were known for producing high-quality and durable footwear suitable for long-distance running. As the company grew, they expanded into other sports and fitness activities including football, basketball, tennis, aerobics, and weightlifting.

## The 80s: The Rise of Reebok

- **Introduction of the Freestyle aerobics shoe:** Targeted towards women, this shoe was designed specifically for aerobics and quickly became a hit.
- **Release of popular shoe models:** The Ex-O-Fit and Classic Leather were known for their classic and stylish design, as well as high-quality construction.

## The 90s: Innovation and Expansion

- **Release of the Pump:** A shoe with inflatable technology that allowed for a customized fit. It was worn by many professional athletes.
- **Expansion into apparel and fitness equipment:** Reebok created sportswear, activewear clothing, and fitness equipment like weightlifting machines and exercise bikes.

## The 21st Century: Reebok Today

- **Acquisition by adidas AG:** In 2005, Reebok was acquired by adidas AG, allowing it to expand its reach and access to new markets.
- **Strong focus on fitness and wellness:** Today, Reebok is known for its comfortable and durable products across running, training, and cross-fit.
- **Presence in the fashion industry:** Through collaborations with designers and celebrities, Reebok has created unique and stylish clothing and footwear.

## Conclusion

Reebok has evolved from a small company focused on running shoes to a global brand known for innovative products, fashionable designs, and a strong focus on fitness and wellness.
