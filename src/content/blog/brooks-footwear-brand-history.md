Established over a century ago, Brooks has made a significant impact on the world of running and footwear, helping athletes and runners achieve their best performance.

## Early Years of Brooks

The history of Brooks dates back to 1914, when Morris Goldenberg founded the company in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Initially, Brooks focused on producing shoes for track and field athletes.

One of the key innovations that set Brooks apart was the **use of kangaroo leather** in its shoes. This material was lighter and more durable than traditional leather. Additionally, Brooks was one of the first companies to use arch supports in its shoes.

## Growth and Development

As Brooks grew, it diversified its product offerings and entered new markets. The company launched new product lines, including shoes specifically designed for women and children, and entered markets in Europe and Asia.

One major milestone was the launch of the BioMoGo midsole in 2004. This innovative midsole was made from a biodegradable material that provided runners with a comfortable and responsive ride while being environmentally friendly.

## The Brooks Philosophy

At the heart of Brooks' success is its unique philosophy centered around "Run Happy." Established in 2011, this philosophy is based on the belief that running should be a joyful and fulfilling experience for everyone, regardless of their ability or experience level.

The Run Happy philosophy is reflected in everything Brooks does, from shoe design to marketing and outreach efforts. The company has a strong community of runners and athletes who are passionate about the sport.

## Legacy and Impact

Over its long history, Brooks has made a significant impact on the world of footwear and running. The company's commitment to innovation, quality, and the needs of runners has helped it establish a reputation as a leader in the industry.

Brooks' legacy can be seen in the countless runners and athletes who have relied on its shoes to perform at their best. The company has helped people of all ages and abilities achieve their running goals.

## Conclusion

The history of Brooks is a story of innovation, quality, and a commitment to the needs of runners. Founded in 1914, the company has been at the forefront of the footwear industry for over a century.
