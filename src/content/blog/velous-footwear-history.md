Hailing from the vibrant city of Portland, Oregon, VELOUS Footwear emerges as a brainchild of industry veterans dedicated to redefining athletic footwear. With a homegrown approach, the company introduces Tri-Motion™ technology and the Foamotion Formula, a patent-pending duo committed to delivering unrivaled comfort and support to athletes. Rooted in the essence of velocity, VELOUS is more than a brand; it’s a celebration of quality movement.

## Visionaries Behind VELOUS

At the helm of VELOUS is CEO Tim Bartels, a luminary with a successful track record in the footwear industry, having played key roles at renowned brands such as SAXX Underwear and Columbia Sportswear. Tim’s leadership sets the tone for VELOUS, ensuring a legacy of excellence and innovation.

As the VP of Product Creation, Brad Bischel brings his expertise to VELOUS, leveraging a proven history of creating footwear styles that resonate with success. His dedication to crafting shoes that seamlessly blend style and functionality reinforces VELOUS as a brand that athletes can trust.

Heading the creative front as VP of Design + Creative, Damon Butler brings a wealth of experience in designing award-winning shoes. His artistic flair infuses VELOUS Footwear with a distinctive identity, ensuring that each pair is a work of both form and function.

## Innovation Takes Center Stage:

Derived from the word ‘velocity,’ VELOUS encapsulates the essence of swift and dynamic movement. The brand’s commitment to optimal comfort and support is embodied in Tri-Motion™ Technology and the Foamotion Formula, promising athletes a game-changing recovery solution.

This commitment to innovation not only enhanced the performance of Velous shoes but also solidified the brand’s reputation as a pioneer in the footwear world.

## Customer-Centric Policies:

In line with their dedication to customer satisfaction, VELOUS Footwear offers a hassle-free 30-day return policy. Encouraging customers to experience their revolutionary recovery solution risk-free, VELOUS aims to build trust and satisfaction one step at a time.

## Conclusion:

VELOUS Footwear, born from the visionary minds of industry veterans in the heart of Oregon, is not just a brand but a testament to the fusion of innovation and performance.

With leaders like Tim Bartels, Brad Bischel, and Damon Butler steering the ship, VELOUS promises a future where athletes can stride confidently, propelled by cutting-edge technology and a commitment to quality movement.

Step into the world of VELOUS, where every pair is crafted for excellence, and experience a new dimension of athletic footwear.
