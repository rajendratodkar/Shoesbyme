Choosing the right shoes to wear on your vacation doesn't have to be difficult, just follow these simple steps and you'll be wearing comfortable, durable and lightweight shoes in no time!

## Comfort is Key

When choosing shoes for vacation, the most important quality is comfort. You want a shoe that will feel good on your feet and not make your feet ache at the end of the day. It's also important to consider how much use your travel shoes will get.

## Consider the Activities You'll Be Doing

It's important to consider where you'll be going and what you'll be doing while there before deciding on a shoe. Activities like hiking, sightseeing and walking on cobblestone streets can put extra stress on your feet and require a more durable shoe with shock absorption.

## Go for Durability

When choosing shoes for vacation, it's important to select something that is both comfortable and durable. However, it's equally as important that your shoes are tough enough to withstand whatever you're going to be doing when you're there. Find shoes that are made from materials like canvas or leather so they can stand up to any adventure.

## Keep an eye on weight

When it comes to packing, weight is one of the most important factors. Shoe weight can vary drastically depending on what type of shoe you choose. Avoid plastic shoes or boots as they tend to be heavy.

## Don't forget about Style

It's important that your shoes are not only comfortable, durable and lightweight but also stylish. You'll be wearing them every day, so make sure you're picking out shoes that will compliment all of your outfits. Choose something versatile like a flat sandal that can go with any outfit.
